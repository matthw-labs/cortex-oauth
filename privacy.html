Cortex · Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Cortex is a personal assistant used only by its developer. It has no other users.

What Cortex accesses

With the owner's consent, Cortex uses two Google permissions on his own account: the events of his calendars (to read them and to create, move or delete events when he asks) and the list of his calendars (read only).

Where the data goes

  • Cortex runs on the owner's own server. The Google access token is kept in a file readable only by the service.
  • To answer the owner's requests, the text of the events (title, time, place, notes, guest names) is sent to the AI model providers he has configured. Meeting links and dial-in codes are removed first.
  • Calendar data is never sold or shared with anyone else, and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions.
